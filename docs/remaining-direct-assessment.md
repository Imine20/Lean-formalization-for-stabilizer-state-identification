## Unformalized alternative: a direct pointwise PGM lower bound

The following is a read-only mathematical check, not a newly proved Lean theorem. It applies to the actual **unweighted** Gram matrix G=S†S of any finite family of normalized pure states, with G≥0 and Gii=1. It requires neither a transitive ensemble, an invertible Gram matrix, nor a constant diagonal of its square root. If the uniform prior has already been absorbed into the Gram matrix, the normalization factor must be converted exactly; diag=1 cannot simply be reused.

Let A=√G and Ei=Σ(j≠i)|Gij|². The argument is as follows:

1. For x≥0, put t=√x. Then 2√x−3x+x²=t(t−1)²(t+2)≥0, so √x≥(3x−x²)/2. This also holds at x=0, allowing singular Gram matrices.
2. Apply continuous functional calculus to the spectrum of G to obtain A≥(3G−G²)/2. Take the i-th diagonal entry. Hermitian symmetry and Gii=1 give (G²)ii=1+Ei, so the real number ai=Aii satisfies ai≥1−Ei/2.
3. If 0≤Ei≤1, then ai≥1/2≥0, hence ai²≥(1−Ei/2)²=1−Ei+Ei²/4≥1−Ei. If Ei≥1, nonnegativity of the actual probability already gives the required lower bound. The case split eliminates Ei≤1 from the conclusion rather than imposing low energy as a hypothesis of the final theorem.
4. The correct probability of the actual supported PGM must separately be proved to equal ai². Concretely, take columns μi=S G^{+1/2} ei, prove Σi μiμi†=Πrange(S), and distribute I−Πrange(S) over the orthogonal complement to form a complete normalized POVM in the original sense. It is also necessary to prove μi†ψi=Aii and that completion on the orthogonal complement does not change the Born probability for any original signal state. This then yields the pointwise bound success_i≥1−Ei.
5. Reuse the result for the family of normalized k-copy pure states, where Ei=Σ(j≠i)|⟨ψi,ψj⟩|^(2k). Substituting the same energy upper bound, valid at every center and supplied by actual sharp Lagrangian shell counting, gives a common worst-case and average-case lower bound without first proving transitivity of the full state orbit or PGM covariance.

The pinned mathlib version provides the PSD order from MatrixOrder, `cfc_mono`/`cfc_nonneg`, and `CFC.sqrt`, which can support steps 1–2. This identifies available foundational interfaces; it is not Lean compilation evidence. Necessary interfaces still include representations of the original density matrices by normalized pure vectors and an actual synthesis map S, singular-support projections, identities for the pseudoinverse square root, and completion to a POVM on the full space. These steps cannot be bypassed by assuming success_i=ai² or the existence of a PGM attaining the desired bound.

The recommended priority is to implement this pointwise bound in the matrix package. It gives a more direct route than first proving full Clifford/phase transitivity, PGM covariance, and equality of all diagonal entries, and can shorten the logical dependencies of Main's direct clause. The optimality and equal-success claims of the paper's `thm:transitive-pure-pgm` remain separate open tasks; no optimality proof is given here. Similarly, even if a sufficiently sharp shell upper bound with constant 4 proves Main's limits, it does not establish the paper's finite direct theorem with its exact κ constant.

## Smallest next acceptance package and dependency order

The recommended smallest independent package is postprocessing for fixed W on all states. Prove that every original state has a legal report, construct a fixed `f : StabilizerState → Report W`, and, for every actual POVM M for complete identification, prove that `POVM.pushforward f M` is an original Learner whose original exactStatewise score is at least the Born probability that M correctly identifies the complete state. The original verification score of the same correct report is one. This package assumes no decoder-error or probability bound, covers exceptional states, m=0, m=n, and p=2, and can be compiled and audited independently. It proves the actual direct reduction rather than creating another coordinate model.

In parallel, divide the substantial remaining gaps into two packages whose work does not share a writable directory:

- Matrix package: a normalized PGM with singular support, the actual Gram identity, and the pointwise overlap-energy error bound above, without a transitivity assumption. This alternative route still needs actual Lean compilation and axiom auditing.
- Geometry package: the actual symplectic quotient and Lagrangian shell count, followed by multiplication by the proved exact phase fiber to obtain the actual overlap energy of complete states.

Once postprocessing, PGM, and shell counting are proved, connect them in this order: finite statewise direct bound → four uniform limits equal to one under the original real-valued overhead → the two achievable-rate infimum equalities → the exact original Main type → dual rebuilds with complete axiom and proof-link audits. Do not make PGM realizability, the Gram identity, shell counting, the pointwise probability bound, or existence of legal reports for all states hypotheses of the later main theorem. With the pointwise route above, Main needs statewise covariance neither as a hypothesis nor as a prerequisite result.
